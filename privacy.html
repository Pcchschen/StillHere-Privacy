Privacy Policy for Nany

Effective date: 2026-05-18

This Privacy Policy explains how Nany collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account and contact details you provide (such as emergency contact email).
  • Location data, including background location, when you enable location features.
  • Status/check-in records and related timestamps that you create in the app.
  • Diagnostic and app usage data needed to maintain reliability and security.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide safety features such as check-ins, alerts, and location updates.
  • To notify your configured emergency contact according to app behavior.
  • To improve app stability, performance, and user experience.

3. Location Data

Nany may process real-time and background location only when permissions are granted by you. You can disable location permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be shared only as needed to deliver app functionality, such as sending alerts to your designated contacts.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed to provide app services and comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Choices

  • You can update your emergency contact and settings inside the app.
  • You can revoke permissions in iOS/Android system settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

Nany is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
